A New Era of Madness. Welcome 2027.
Prepared by Advara Event Management | Version 1.0 | November 2026
1. Executive Summary
ERA 27 is Advara Event Management’s New Year’s Eve flagship: a large-scale, multi-level LED stage experience on 31 December 2026, 6 PM at Aziz Nagar, Moinabad, Hyderabad. It combines live concert-style performances across languages, a dedicated “Surprise Your Loved Ones” stage, a full food and fun stall zone, and a 21+ bar counter, for an expected 1,500 guests.
This document defines the event concept, brand identity, offerings, commercial model, operations scope and the digital deliverables: a promotional popup on the existing Advara website and a dedicated ERA 27 microsite at /era27. It is the single reference for clients, sponsors, investors and the build team.
Event in one line: One stage. Every language. Every vibe. Dance. Celebrate. Go mad.

3. Vision, Goals and Success Metrics
3.1 Vision
To make ERA 27 the most talked-about New Year’s Eve night in Hyderabad: a concert-grade production that feels like the start of a new era, with experiences guests cannot find at a typical NYE party.
3.2 Business Goals
• Sell out or approach the 1,500-guest capacity through pre-booking and official ticket launch.
• Secure title, zone and in-kind sponsors and, where applicable, investors.
• Sell all 20 Surprise Stage slots (5 reserved for Elite Pass guests).
• Establish ERA 27 as a repeatable annual property under the Advara brand.
• Drive wallet-money adoption so guests pre-book food stalls and spend on site.
3.3 Digital Goals
• Convert homepage visitors into ERA 27 microsite visitors via the popup.
• Convert microsite visitors into pass buyers, Surprise Stage enquiries and sponsor leads.
• Provide a single, mobile-first source of truth for event information.

4. Target Audience
• Young adults and professionals (18–35) seeking a concert-style NYE with music, food and photo moments.
• Couples who want to plan a public surprise for a partner.
• Friend groups and families buying Duo, Trio, Squad or Mega Squad passes.
• Premium guests who want VIP treatment through the Elite Pass.
• Brands and investors looking for visibility with a large, engaged NYE crowd.

12. Technical Approach
• Stack: Next.js (App Router), React, TypeScript (existing project).
• Files to add: components/Era27Popup.tsx, app/era27/page.tsx, app/era27/layout.tsx (metadata and isolated wrapper), app/era27/era27.module.css (scoped styles), components/era27/* (section components), lib/era27-content.ts (single source of truth for event data), public/era27-poster.png.
• Files to touch (minimal): app/page.tsx only to render <Era27Popup />.
• Session logic: read and write sessionStorage only inside useEffect, with try/catch, so server rendering and private-browsing modes do not break.
• RSVP backend: to be decided (email via existing enquiry mechanism, Google Sheet, or a payment/ticketing provider). See open decisions.
